Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I notice sparks or a burning smell?

Keep away from the affected area. If it is safe, switch off the main power and contact a qualified electrician. If there is a fire or immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Can I request a quote before work begins?

Yes. Ask for the issue to be inspected and the scope and price to be explained before authorising any work.

Do you provide new wiring and repair services?

The service categories shown here include household wiring, repairs, lighting, switches, switchboards and safety checks. Confirm availability and coverage before booking.
